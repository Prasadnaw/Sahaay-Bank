⚡ CORE TECHNOLOGY

Multi-Turn Conversational Voice Assistant & UPI Engine

Natural language payments with echo cancellation and two-step safety confirmation.

Conversational Transfer Flow
👤 User: "Send money"
🤖 Sahaay: "Who would you like to send money to? Say a name, phone, or UPI ID."
👤 User: "Rahul"
🤖 Sahaay: "How much money would you like to send to Rahul?"
👤 User: "500 rupees"
🛡️ Safety Check: "Are you sure you want to transfer ₹500 to Rahul? Say Yes to confirm."
Smart Audio Engineering
  • Intelligent Spoken UPI Normalizer: Converts spoken words like "rahul at the rate o k a x i s" to rahul@okaxis.
  • Dual-Channel Echo Protection: Mic automatically pauses during TTS speech with a 750ms cooldown to eliminate acoustic self-trigger loops.
  • Hands-Free Auto-Rearm: Continuous listening lifecycle keeps the mic active for blind users without manual re-clicks.
  • Contextual Error Recovery: Never kicks users to an error page on unrecognized speech; stays on the current screen and offers clear hints.
