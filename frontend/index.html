📚 Banking Terminologies Glossary

Term Simple Plain Explanation
UPI ID / VPA Virtual Payment Address (e.g. asha.patel@sahaay). A simple nickname for your bank account so you never need to share account numbers.
UPI PIN A 4-digit secret security code you enter on the on-screen keypad to authorize and release money. Never share this with anyone!
Account Freeze A 1-tap emergency lock that stops all outgoing money and card transactions instantly if you suspect fraud or lost a card.
Beneficiary A trusted person or merchant whose payment details you have saved for fast, verified 1-tap payments.
Available Balance The exact money currently in your account ready to spend right now, minus pending holds.
